## 💡 The Problem

Knowing today's mandi price is not enough.

A farmer still has to decide:

> **Should I sell today, wait for a better price, sell only part of my harvest, or choose another nearby market?**

Price differences between nearby mandis, changing trends, limited market visibility, and language barriers can make this decision difficult.

**KisanLink turns market information into a simple, actionable selling strategy.**

---

## 🚜 How KisanLink Helps

```text
Crop & Location
      ↓
Market Prices
      ↓
7-Day Trend + Nearby Mandi Comparison
      ↓
AI-Assisted Market Advisory
      ↓
SELL • HOLD • SPLIT
      ↓
Buyer Discovery + Income Estimate
```

Instead of only showing **what the price is**, KisanLink helps answer **what the farmer can do next**.

## 🧠 Explainable Decision Support

KisanLink does not simply display **"HOLD"** or **"SELL."**

It explains the factors behind the recommendation:

**Price Trend** → **Nearby Market Spread** → **Market Conditions** → **Suggested Action**

For example:

> **HOLD PARTIALLY — 74% confidence**  
> Sell **40% now** for immediate liquidity and hold **60%** based on the demonstrated upward market scenario.

This makes the recommendation easier to understand instead of treating AI as a black box.

---

## 🔵 IBM Granite Vision

**IBM Granite** is positioned as the intelligence layer for KisanLink's Market Advisor:

```text
Market Context → IBM Granite → Explainable Advisory → Farmer Action
```

For this **Round 1 frontend MVP**, recommendations use deterministic demonstration models so the complete farmer journey can be evaluated without external API dependencies.

A production version can connect the same workflow to **IBM watsonx.ai / Granite** with live market data for dynamic reasoning.

## 🔍 Prototype Transparency

KisanLink is currently a **hackathon MVP**.

Market prices and trends are demonstration datasets, buyer profiles are samples, and advisory outputs use prototype decision models. The current version is **not connected to live APMC data or a production IBM Granite endpoint**.

Future development can introduce live market feeds, Granite inference, verified buyer onboarding, Gujarati voice interaction, FPO integration, and personalized recommendations.
